FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our courses, batches and enrollment.

What courses does AI Masters Academy offer?

AI Masters Academy offers six courses: Python Full-Stack Development, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Data Science, Cyber Security, UI/UX Designing and Digital Marketing.

How long are the courses?

Python Full-Stack Development, Data Science and Cyber Security run for about 6 months. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning runs for about 7 months. UI/UX Designing and Digital Marketing run for about 4 months.

Do I need prior experience to join?

Python Full-Stack Development and Data Science are beginner friendly. UI/UX Designing and Digital Marketing need no prior experience. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning needs basic Python, and basic networking knowledge helps for Cyber Security.

What will I learn in the Python Full-Stack course?

You learn Python, HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, SQL and PostgreSQL, Django, REST APIs with Django REST Framework, React, testing with pytest, Docker, CI/CD and cloud deployment, and finish with a capstone project.

Which tools and technologies are taught in the AI and Machine Learning course?

Python, NumPy, Pandas, Jupyter, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, OpenCV, Hugging Face, FastAPI and Docker, covering machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, generative AI and MLOps.

What study modes and career support are available?

You can choose classroom, live online or weekend batches. Career support includes resume and LinkedIn help, portfolio reviews, mock interviews and guidance on job applications.
